# Melodan – Descriptions

## One line

Melodan is a medieval fantasy strategy auto-battler: deploy your army in secret, then watch the battle play out.

## Short (about 50 words)

Melodan is a medieval fantasy strategy auto-battler. Each round, both players recruit units, place them on the battlefield and pick their spells – hidden from each other. Then the armies clash on their own. Read your opponent, place the perfect counter and adapt round after round until one commander falls.

## Long (about 250 words)

Melodan is a medieval fantasy strategy auto-battler about outthinking, not outclicking.

Every round has two phases. In deployment, both players secretly build their army: swarms of goblins, stalwart dwarven infantry, archers, crow riders, wizards, ogres, ballistas and towering dragons. Units are upgraded, given talents and runes, and placed on the grid next to walls, gates and towers. Spells like a war band of dwarves bursting from the ground or a dragon's fire breath can turn a battle.

Then the fight starts – fully automatic. Units march, clash and cast on their own, and the plan decides who wins. Surviving units damage the enemy commander; the first commander to fall loses the match. Round by round, both sides see what the other built and adapt.

Melodan is played 1v1 or 2v2 against other players, ranked or casual, or against relentless waves of AI-controlled undead in Horde and Co-op Horde mode. Ever-changing round cards and modifiers keep every match different.

Every match is recorded: replays can be watched from any player's view, and a built-in recorder turns any battle into a cinematic video with an automatic battle camera.

## Features

- Secret deployment, automatic battles: strategy, not reflexes
- Dozens of fantasy units, from goblins and dwarves to ogres and dragons
- Walls, gates and towers to build and defend
- Spells, round cards, talents and runes to adapt every round
- 1v1 and 2v2 PvP, Horde and Co-op Horde against AI waves
- Replays of every match with a cinematic replay recorder
- Free in the browser, full game on Steam, 31 languages, open source

## The story behind it

Melodan is made by Alexander Thurn, an indie game developer from Germany who has been making games since 1998 – because he loves it. A big fan of the strategy auto-battler Mechabellum, he set out to make a medieval fantasy take on the genre. He thought it would take one prompt. That was more than five months ago, and the game is now heading for its Steam release in March 2027. Melodan is developed with the help of AI coding tools, and its source code is open.
