**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

### Medieval fantasy auto-battler Melodan opens its free playtest on Steam

*[City], Germany – [Date]* – Indie developer Alexander Thurn today opened the free playtest of **Melodan**, a medieval fantasy strategy auto-battler, on Steam. The full release is planned for March 2027.

In Melodan, both players secretly recruit and place their army each round – goblins, dwarves, archers, ogres, dragons and more – behind walls, gates and towers. Then the battle plays out automatically, and the better plan wins. Matches are played 1v1 or 2v2, or against waves of AI-controlled undead in Horde mode.

"[Quote: why you made Melodan, in one or two sentences.]" says Alexander Thurn, who has been making games since 1998.

**Key features**
- Secret deployment, automatic battles: strategy, not reflexes
- Dozens of fantasy units, spells, round cards, talents and runes
- 1v1 and 2v2 PvP, Horde and Co-op Horde
- Replays with a cinematic replay recorder
- Free in the browser, 31 languages, open source
