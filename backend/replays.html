"Verified" means at least one ⚡ Verify re-check (a headless re-simulation of the stored action log) has been run against this match and nothing it touched disagreed — with itself, or with the opposing side. "Unverified" just means no one has run Verify on it yet (solo matches — only one side ever submitted — count the same as paired ones here; whether both sides reported is shown in the score line, not the badge). "Mismatch" means a disagreement was found — between two records on the same side, between the two sides, or between an original submission and a later Verify.
