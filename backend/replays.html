"Consistent" means every record on each side agrees with itself, and the two sides report opposite results (or both draw) with matching round counts — the minimum you'd expect from honest clients replaying the same match. It is not a full divergence check (that needs actually re-simulating each side's action log) — just a cheap, immediate sanity flag on the summary fields already submitted. A "verify" tag marks a later headless re-check (see the ⚡ Verify link) rather than the match's own original report — extra verify records only appear here when they disagree with something, since a matching one dedupes away server-side.
