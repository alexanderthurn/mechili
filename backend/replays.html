Every real client submits its own replay at match end — records sharing a matchKey are the same match (plus any later Verify re-checks).

"other build" means the replay was recorded with another version (or other unit data) than the game this page opens: Watch then plays it under today's rules, so the battles come out differently, and Verify is off for it. "quit" means the player gave up or left before the end. "Verified" means at least one ⚡ Verify re-check (a headless re-simulation of the stored action log) has been run against this match and nothing it touched disagreed — with itself, or with the opposing side. "Unverified" just means no one has run Verify on it yet (solo matches — only one side ever submitted — count the same as paired ones here; whether both sides reported is shown in the score line, not the badge). "Mismatch" means a disagreement was found — between two records on the same side, between the two sides, or between an original submission and a later Verify. Click a row to expand it: it lists every Verify run so far (who, when, match/mismatch), plus the full raw record per submission.
