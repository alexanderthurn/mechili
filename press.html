About Melodan

Melodan is a medieval fantasy strategy auto-battler about outthinking, not outclicking.

Every round has two phases. In deployment, both players secretly build their army: swarms of goblins, stalwart dwarven infantry, archers, crow riders, wizards, ogres, ballistas and towering dragons. Units are upgraded, given talents and runes, and placed on the grid next to walls, gates and towers. Spells like a war band of dwarves bursting from the ground or a dragon's fire breath can turn a battle.

Then the fight starts – fully automatic. Units march, clash and cast on their own, and the plan decides who wins. Surviving units damage the enemy commander; the first commander to fall loses the match. Round by round, both sides see what the other built and adapt.

Melodan is played 1v1 or 2v2 against other players, ranked or casual, or against relentless waves of AI-controlled undead in Horde and Co-op Horde mode. Every match is recorded: replays can be watched from any player's view, and a built-in recorder turns any battle into a cinematic video.

Features

  • Secret deployment, automatic battles: strategy, not reflexes
  • Dozens of fantasy units, from goblins and dwarves to ogres and dragons
  • Walls, gates and towers to build and defend
  • Spells, round cards, talents and runes to adapt every round
  • 1v1 and 2v2 PvP, Horde and Co-op Horde against AI waves
  • Replays of every match with a cinematic replay recorder
  • Free in the browser, full game on Steam, 31 languages, open source

FAQ

When does Melodan release?
19 March 2027 on Steam.
Can I play it now?
Yes. The free playtest has been open on Steam since 1 November 2026, and the game is free to play in the browser at play.melodan.com.
Where can players see it next?
At the Steam Auto Battler RPG Fest 2026, 16–23 November 2026.
How much will it cost?
The price is not announced yet. The game is open source (GPL-3.0).
Which platforms?
PC via Steam (Windows, macOS, Linux) and the browser. No console versions are planned.
Does it run on Steam Deck?
No, Steam Deck is not supported.
Is there crossplay between the browser and Steam?
No. Browser players and Steam players play separately.
Who makes Melodan?
Alexander Thurn, a solo indie developer from Germany, working with AI coding tools. TNT, the first new team member, takes care of the community.
What is it built with?
three.js for the 3D battlefield, running in the browser; the Steam version is a desktop build of the same game. The source code is open.
May I stream it or use the footage?
Yes – streams and videos are welcome, monetized ones included, and screenshots and footage may be used freely for coverage. No need to ask.

About the developer

Alexander Thurn is an indie game developer from Germany who has been making games since 1998 – because he loves it. A big fan of the strategy auto-battler Mechabellum, he set out to make a medieval fantasy take on the genre. He thought it would take one prompt. That was more than five months ago, and Melodan is now heading for its Steam release in March 2027.
